## Hard comparison rules

1. Compare atomic claims only when both have source-specific precise locators. A model sounding plausible does not count.
2. Confirm source revisions and lineages first; source #001/#002, advisory+technical pairs, and aggregator articles must not become independent individual discoveries.
3. Review every inferred question/experiment/causal edge separately from investigator-authored retrospection.
4. Negative and inconclusive evidence must preserve test scope and the distinction between not observed vs refuted.
5. Historical predecision snapshots require contemporaneous knowable-by-cutoff evidence: publication date alone is insufficient.
6. Human independent adjudication remains required before promotion to canonical RDL registries or HOMELAB knowledge.
