**Scope:** complete title/metadata triage of all 109 registry source entries, not a reading or source verification of all 109 articles. Only sources 011, 092, 104 were randomly selected and their original pages inspected for this round. All classifications besides those three are hypotheses requiring full-text confirmation. **Universal requirements for every source:** original-access and rights review; source-version/provenance; atomic claims; claim-evidence verification; independent scientific review; no direct promotion of a proposed reconstruction.

## Processing markers (non-exclusive, not engines or a router)

- **T** — Research-state reconstruction: questions, trials, decisions and pivots.
- **K** — Technical mechanism: code, configurations, dataflow and preconditions.
- **C** — Causal explanations and competing interpretations.
- **N** — Negative, blocked and inconclusive experiment semantics.
- **Q** — Quantitative methodology, denominators, metrics and sampling bias.
- **L** — Researcher/actor identity, parallel runs, lineage and independent-source status.
- **D** — Disclosure, vendor response, fix and public-knowledge timing.
- **P** — Methodology, policy or conceptual argument; do not fabricate a discovery trajectory.
- **F** — Forensic/reproduction study of prior discovery; separate original actor from analyst.

## How to use this map safely

1. The markers are **source treatment requirements**, not exclusive classification. A page may require multiple analyses for different evidence segments.
2. The document is **not an adjudicated 109-case audit**: follow-up source reading can add/remove every non-random-row marker.
3. Do not assume a report, a ranked top-10 list or vendor advisory contains original discovery decision history.
4. Aggregate benchmarks and original individual event trajectories must not be conflated.
5. Any missing original RAW, log timestamp, author, independent run, or explicit transition motive remains unknown.
