# Pentera Labs source integration

Source discovery: https://pentera.io/resources/ → Research filter → pages 1–2, verified 2026-10-16.

- 22 publisher-labelled research publications catalogued: 11 methodology/conceptual items and 11 other technical/security investigations or architectural discussions (these are *preliminary*, not reviewed research classifications).
- Publisher-labelled **Research** includes theoretical AI essays. Do not train a discovery-trajectory model from those essays as though they document real investigation timelines.
- Sales datasheets, demos, customer stories and SANS product review were deliberately excluded.
- Article URL and listing date were inspected. Dates shown as YYYY-MM represent **month-level precision**, not the first or last day of a month.
- RAW original bytes, PDFs, screenshots and article contents were **not** copied. The 22 sources have `capture_status=not_archived`; public access does not imply redistribution permission.
- `CASE-000055` is a *partial manual reconstruction* of [ShadowPanel](https://pentera.io/resources/research/pre-auth-rce-chain-cyberpanel/); all other imported CASE records remain unreviewed catalogue placeholders.
- Reconstructed assumptions/questions are explicitly tagged `origin=reconstructed` / `evidence_status=proposed`. Historically described directions, failed attempts and pivots cite the publisher article's named sections. No experiment was independently repeated.
- The absence of exact event times must not be papered over with invented timestamps. Follow source-backed order/causal links instead.

This is a bounded source import, **not** an unattended web crawler or a license to mirror Pentera pages.
