# Archive policy

The project is public. Cataloguing a publication is distinct from republishing its copyrighted full text.

- Preserve public source URLs and attribution.
- Before copying full RAW: check license, permission, distribution constraints, and potential sensitive data.
- For authorized RAW captures, record SHA-256, retrieval timestamp, original URL, content type, capture tool/version, license/permission, and storage location.
- Keep private/licensed captures in access-controlled storage when redistribution is not permitted.
- Keep all analysis and model-generated reconstructions separate from original source bytes.
- Never invent a discovery timeline or claim a hypothesis was historically asked unless explicitly documented.
- Deduplicate by canonical source URL and content hash; maintain lineage and versions.
- Holdouts must be isolated by research family and source lineage, not just random records.
